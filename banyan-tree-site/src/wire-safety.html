Wire safety policy

Call before you wire. Every time.

Wire fraud is the most common way people lose money in a real estate deal. The fix is simple and it is a phone call.

Our number is {{phone}}. Use it to confirm wire instructions before you send any money. Do not use a phone number from an email.

What we do

  • We never change wire instructions by email. If an email says our instructions changed, it is fraud.
  • We give wire instructions by phone or through a secure link, never as a plain email attachment.
  • Before we send money out, a person in our office confirms the receiving account by phone, using a number we already have on file.
  • Payoffs, seller proceeds, and commissions all follow the same rule. No exceptions for a rush.

What you should do

  • Call us at {{phone}} before you wire. Read the account number back to us.
  • Be suspicious of any message that is urgent, changes the plan, or asks you to keep it quiet.
  • Check the sender's full email address. Fraud emails often change one letter.
  • After you send the wire, call us to confirm we received it.

If you think you wired money to the wrong account

  1. Call your bank's fraud department right now and ask for a wire recall. Minutes matter.
  2. Call us at {{phone}}.
  3. Report it to the FBI at ic3.gov.
