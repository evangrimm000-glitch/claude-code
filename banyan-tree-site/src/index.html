Fees

The premium is set by the state. The service is not.

Florida sets the price of title insurance by rule. Every agency charges the same premium for the same policy. What you are choosing is who does the work.

When you buy with a loan, the lender's policy issued alongside your owner's policy costs $25 for coverage up to the purchase price.

Questions

What people ask us.

Not here? Call {{phone}}.

What does a title company actually do?

We check the public record to make sure the seller can sell and nothing is attached to the property that should not be. We fix what we find, hold the money, run the closing, record the deed, and issue the insurance policy that protects your ownership.

How much does title insurance cost in Florida?

The state sets the rate. On a $400,000 purchase the owner's policy is $2,075. You pay it once, at closing, and it covers you for as long as you own the property. Use the calculator for your number.

Who picks the title company?

Your contract decides. In most of Tampa Bay the seller pays for the owner's policy and picks the agency. In some Florida counties the buyer does. Either way, you or your agent can ask for us by name.

How long does a closing take?

A cash purchase with clean title can close in about a week. A purchase with a loan usually runs on the lender's clock, 30 to 45 days.

Can I close if I am not in Florida?

Yes. We send you the documents with instructions. You sign in front of a notary where you are and send them back.

Do you handle assignments and double closings?

Yes. Tell us the structure when you open the order so the file is set up correctly from day one.
